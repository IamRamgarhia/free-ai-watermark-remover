Remove AI watermarks without uploading your files

The AI model runs inside this browser tab using WebAssembly, so the picture you are editing never leaves your device. No upload, no account, no queue — there is no server that could keep a copy, because there is no server.

Most watermarks from image generators are semi-transparent, which means the picture underneath them still exists. Rather than painting over the badge and inventing new pixels, WatermarkOut solves for what was behind it, so texture and detail survive.
