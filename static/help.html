Help

Common questions, answered plainly.

How do I remove a watermark?

Drop in an image or short clip, paint over the watermark (or choose a preset for common corner marks), then click Remove watermark. Download the result when it finishes.

Why is the first run slow?

The AI model (~29 MB) downloads once and is cached on your device. Subsequent runs are fast and can work offline.

Does my file get uploaded anywhere?

Not by default — the app runs in your browser tab and your files stay local. An optional cloud-quality mode (images only) will send the image to a processing function; it is not stored afterward.

My video watermark still shows partway through the clip.

Video mode only removes a watermark that stays in a fixed position throughout the clip. If the mark moves, appears partway through, or the camera pans, it won't be tracked.

The result looks blurry or smudged.

Try painting a tighter mask around the watermark. The less the model has to invent, the cleaner the result. Complex backgrounds (faces, fine text, patterns) remain challenging for inpainting models.

What file types and sizes are supported?

Images: JPG, PNG, WEBP, BMP. Videos: MP4, WEBM, MOV. Files up to ~100 MB; video works best under ~30 seconds.

Nothing happens when I click Remove watermark.

The button remains disabled until a mask is drawn. If it still doesn't respond, ensure your browser supports WebAssembly and Service Workers (recent Chrome, Edge, Firefox, or Safari 16.4+).

Can I use this on content I don't own?

No — use this tool only on files you own or have permission to edit. See the Legal page for details.
