👋 About me

I’m Chaitanya Lalit Patil, and I built FreeHub Pro Watermark Remover because I wanted a simple tool that helps creators remove distracting AI watermarks while keeping everything local and private.

The idea is straightforward: you upload a file, paint over the watermark, and the AI fills it in in your browser. Your file stays on your device unless you choose an optional cloud mode.

🎯 Why this exists

Many online watermark removers charge a subscription, ask you to upload private files to a server, or add their own branding. This tool is a more honest alternative: local by default, lightweight, and focused on a single job.

⚙️ How it works

  1. You drop in an image or short clip. The file is processed in your browser tab.
  2. You paint over the watermark. A mask marks the area to be repaired.
  3. An AI inpainting model runs locally. It fills the marked area and returns a cleaned result.
  4. You download the finished file. No handoff to a third-party server is required in default mode.

🧾 Honest limits

This tool works best when the watermark is clear and stays in one place. It may struggle with fast-moving marks, complex backgrounds, faces, or fine text. Please use it only on content you own or have the right to edit.
