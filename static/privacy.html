Privacy

Your files stay private by default.

In your browser (default)

When you use the default mode, your image or video is decoded and processed entirely inside this browser tab. It is never uploaded, never sent to a server, and no copy of it is retained by the app.

Cloud quality mode (optional, images only)

If you opt into cloud quality mode for images, the image and the painted mask are sent to a processing function to run a higher-quality model. The image is processed and returned; it is not stored afterward.

Ads

This site may show ads to cover hosting costs. Ad providers may use their own cookies or tracking as described in their privacy policies.

Data retention & telemetry

The app itself does not collect or retain your files. Minimal, anonymous telemetry may be used to diagnose critical failures; no personal data is intentionally collected. If you opt into cloud processing, that request is transient and the image is not kept.
